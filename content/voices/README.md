# Voices remembering slavery

Three continuous excerpts for `wpa-narratives`, streamed from the Library of
Congress. `voices.yaml` records the sources, recording details, rights statement,
credit lines, birth notes, and selected boundaries. No audio is bundled here.

## Playback and subtitles

`start` and `end` are seconds on the original MP3 clock. **WebVTT timestamps use
that same absolute clock**, so the tracks attach directly to the full source
MP3. Seek to `start` and stop at `end`; if a player instead uses an excerpt-local
clock, subtract `start` from every cue. English and Russian tracks have identical
cue IDs and times. WebVTT voice tags identify the speaker.

The selected audio excludes the racial slur present elsewhere in the source
recordings. Smalley's excerpt starts after the preceding sentence containing it;
McCrea's ends after “They all going home now,” before the enslaver's quoted speech.
These are single intervals, without internal edits. Smalley's final repeated
“celebrates that day” is outside the excerpt. Signal and recognition checks found
no repeating groove at 09:10–09:40.

English follows the LoC transcript, including its standardized wording and
editorial conventions, rather than replacing it with recognition output. The
selected intervals contain no third-party surnames needing initials; the
excluded McCrea passage is printed as “Col. M.” in the source. Russian follows
the research draft, divided by the same meanings. The uncertain sense of
Hughes's “Night never comed out” is retained as the draft's “Ночь не ночь [?]”.
Laughter and the trailing voice are source transcript annotations.

## Source precision

Hughes's June 11, 1949 date follows the LoC item title and recording note; its
`created_published` field inconsistently says November. Smalley's and McCrea's
dates are known only to the year. Hughes's claimed age of 101 is not used to
infer his birth year. Smalley's exact birth year is unknown; McCrea's approximate
birth year follows his own age reckoning, not his competing claim of 117.

The current collection rights statement quoted in `voices.yaml` is the source
for reuse. The XML transcripts also retain a legacy “Copyright status not
determined” header. Credit each excerpt with its individual `credit` value.
