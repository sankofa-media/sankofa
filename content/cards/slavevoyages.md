SlaveVoyages is a free online database of the transatlantic slave trade, built by historians who gathered the records of individual slave ships from archives on both sides of the Atlantic, led among others by David Eltis of Emory University. In 1999 the first version came out on a CD-ROM of 27,233 voyages, and on 5 December 2008 Emory launched it as a free website covering nearly 35,000. Behind the voyages stands the scale: between 1500 and 1866 traders forced 12.5 million Africans aboard. Its African Origins database goes further. It lists nearly 100,000 Africans liberated from slave ships by their own names, and asks visitors to help trace where they came from, since a name can point to a homeland. In 2025 Harvard's Hutchins Center announced that the project would make its permanent home there.
