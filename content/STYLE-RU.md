# Russian style of the Sankofa museum

This applies to all Russian text in the museum: cards, chapters, longreads, help, buttons, factoids. It sits on top of `TRANSLATION-RU.md` and `GLOSSARY-RU.md` and takes precedence over them on style.

## Models

- **Nora Gal, "The Word Living and Dead".** A living word instead of officialese and calques. No "является", "осуществляет", "данный", "в рамках", "играл роль в", "был частью", "в то время как"-style mirroring of English.
- **Artemy Lebedev, "Kovodstvo".** Clarity, honesty with the reader, correct typography: «ёлочки» and nested „лапки“, an em dash with spaces, a non-breaking space after short words and between a number and its unit.
- **Tone.** Meduza, and Vedomosti before 2010: calm, intelligent, slightly ironic prose for an adult who is not a specialist. Not academic, not promotional, not "storytelling".

## Pace (the owner's main request)

**A little more epic, a little slower and smoother, more literary, more connected, and even a little redundant with information.**

- **Don't chop.** A sentence may be long if it reads in one breath. Join sentences with links: "поэтому", "и всё же", "тем временем", "через три года", "а в это время".
- **Give context before the event.** Where, when, what the world around was like, who these people are. Remind the reader of a name or idea if it hasn't been mentioned for a while, rather than relying on the reader's memory.
- **Explain why.** Not "the National Guard was called" but why it was called and what was feared. Every explanation must rest on the sources; don't make up details for the sake of beauty.
- **Redundancy is a virtue here.** Better to say once more who someone is and why it matters than to leave the reader to guess. But don't repeat the same phrase: say it in other words, from a new angle.
- **Rhythm.** Vary sentence length. After a long, unhurried sentence a short one can land: it works as an accent, not as a habit.

## What is forbidden

- **Infostyle.** Chopped phrases with no links, lists instead of thought, "X — this is Y. Z did W." in a row.
- **Meta-disclaimers** for the reader ("on this page all versions are…", "the sources differ…") where you can simply tell the story. A disagreement between sources goes in the card's `dispute` field or into the witnesses' own words.
- **Translator's calques** (see Nora Gal): English word order, "was able to", "it is worth noting", "that said", passive constructions where Russian would use an active verb.
- **A pronoun far from its antecedent.** "Их", "её", "он" only where it is obvious who is meant; otherwise name the person again.
- **Officialese:** "в целях", "посредством", "является", "осуществлять", "имеет место".
- **Exclamation marks and emotional adjectives** in place of facts ("потрясающий", "невероятный"). The pathos comes from the facts.

## Example (the Scottsboro card)

**Was** (correct, but compressed):
> В Скоттсборо, окружном центре, для охраны тюрьмы вызвали Национальную гвардию. За считаные дни белые присяжные осудили восьмерых и приговорили их к смерти…

**Should be** (the same facts, but slower and connected):
> Юношей привезли в Скоттсборо, окружной центр, и посадили в местную тюрьму. Охранять её пришлось Национальной гвардии: штат ждал самосуда. Суд, который последовал, трудно назвать судом. Всего за несколько дней белые присяжные выслушали обвинение, осудили восьмерых из девяти и приговорили их к смерти — восьмерых подростков и молодых людей, которых ещё весной никто в Алабаме не знал по именам.

(The phrase "штат ждал самосуда" stays only if the sources say so; otherwise write only what they confirm.)

## Volume

Redundancy needs room. The card body limit is 320 words in English (340 for genres, languages, scripts and instruments), up to 1.3× that in Russian: the owner raised it on 17.11.2026 for context and connections. Don't cut the connections to fit.
