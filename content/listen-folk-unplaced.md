# Folk recordings not placed in listen.yaml

chapter, no card, so they cannot join `cards.<card-id>` in
`content/listen.yaml`. The listening schema (`web/src/lib/listen.ts`) keys
recordings to cards and roots artists only; a chapter-level section would
parse, but nothing in the museum plays recordings by chapter. They are listed
here until a chapter listening hook exists.

Provenance, sources and rights for every entry: `content/listen-folk.yaml`.
